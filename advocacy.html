Why this is here.

Malan syndrome is a rare genetic disorder associated with changes in the NFIX gene. It can affect development, communication, vision and hearing, growth, the skeleton and other aspects of health. Because the condition is rare, awareness among professionals and access to informed support matter enormously.

My family and I are members of the Malan Syndrome Foundation and ASSI Gulliver. Both organisations connect families, share information and support research and better care.

What helps
01

Recognition

Rare conditions are easier to support when they are recognised. Better awareness can shorten the route to diagnosis and help schools, clinicians and communities understand what families are dealing with.

02

Community

Family networks matter: they allow practical knowledge to travel quickly between people facing similar questions, while reducing the isolation that often comes with an ultra-rare diagnosis.

03

Research

Natural-history studies, registries and clinical research are essential when the known population is small. They build the evidence needed for better guidance, support and future treatments.

04

Inclusion

Support should be designed around participation, communication and dignity. Good inclusion is not simply access to a place; it is access to the relationships, learning and experiences happening there.
