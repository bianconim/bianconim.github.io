03 / Research

Research

My research has centred on galaxies and galaxy clusters: how environment changes galaxies, how clusters trace dark matter, and how lensing can open new windows on the Universe.

Observational astrophysics
Galaxy evolution
Gravitational lensing

Structure, environment and light bent by gravity.

Most of my research sits at the intersection of galaxy evolution and galaxy-cluster astrophysics. Clusters are useful laboratories because gravity, gas physics and galaxy evolution all operate together — and because the same massive structures can act as gravitational lenses.

My work has used multi-wavelength observations, spectroscopy, X-ray data and gravitational-lensing techniques, alongside collaborations connected to gravitational-wave astronomy and large spectroscopic surveys.

Research themes
01

Environment & galaxy evolution

Using cluster galaxies to understand how ram-pressure stripping, group pre-processing and gravitational interactions change star formation and gas supply. Work with LoCuSS showed both enhanced instability and suppressed future gas accretion in galaxies entering dense environments.

Pre-processing paper
02

Strongly lensed gravitational waves

I worked on rapid follow-up strategies for gravitational-wave events whose apparent masses could be altered by strong lensing, including observing campaigns, cluster watch-lists and interpretation of candidate mass-gap detections.

Mass-gap lensing paper
03

Dark-matter haloes & splashback

With spectroscopically confirmed cluster members extending to large radius, we studied the splashback feature and its dependence on cluster dynamical state and accretion history — connecting halo growth to structure across very different scales.

Splashback paper
04

Large surveys & stellar populations

More recent collaborations include WEAVE-StePS work on stellar populations and machine-learning recovery of galaxy properties, together with THE THREE HUNDRED project on the relation between cluster mass and cosmic-web filaments.

Three Hundred / A&A 2024
05

Black holes across the Hubble sequence

Earlier work examined how commonly massive black holes occur in galaxies of very different morphology, including bulgeless and dwarf systems, and how black-hole mass relates to the host galaxy.

Black-hole demographics
06

Intracluster-medium dynamics

I also worked on bulk gas motions and plasma instabilities in the intracluster medium, including the detectability of cluster rotation in X-rays and the possible role of magnetorotational instability in cool cores.

Gas rotation paper
