02 / Teaching

Teaching

I teach physics as a subject built from models, evidence and good questions — not just a collection of equations to remember.

Classroom practice
Physics education

Keep the physics intact.

My route into teaching came after years of research, so I am particularly interested in how we make difficult ideas accessible without making them intellectually thin. The goal is not to remove complexity, but to sequence it so that pupils can actually reason with it.

That means explicit modelling, careful use of representations, frequent checks for understanding and practical work that is treated as evidence rather than theatre. I also want pupils to see where school physics connects to the way scientists actually think.

A good explanation should make the next question possible.

What matters to me
01

Modelling

Working through unfamiliar problems visibly: selecting a model, stating assumptions, choosing a representation and checking whether the result makes physical sense.

02

Responsive teaching

Using hinge questions, mini-whiteboards and short checks to find out what pupils actually understand, then changing the next step rather than simply continuing the planned script.

03

Practical physics

Measurement, uncertainty and data analysis are opportunities to think scientifically. I want pupils to understand why a method works, what the limitations are and how the evidence supports a conclusion.

04

High expectations

Clarity and challenge are compatible. I use scaffolding to get pupils into difficult material, then remove it so that they can work independently and explain their reasoning.

Current interests

Areas I keep returning to.

  • A-level mechanics, oscillations and waves
  • Teaching uncertainty and practical data analysis
  • Conceptual electricity and fields
  • Using astronomy and astrophysics for enrichment
  • Question design that exposes misconceptions rather than rewarding guessing

I am gradually adding selected teaching resources and notes to this site. The aim is to share material that is useful because of the thinking behind it, not simply because it fills a lesson.
